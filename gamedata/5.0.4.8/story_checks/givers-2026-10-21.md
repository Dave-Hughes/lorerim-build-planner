# Six givers to fix or prove (WO-121a Part 3, PC, 21 Oct 2026)

For each line: the dialogue that sets the opening quest's first stage, who says it, where they stand, the player's line and the reply, from the start check (`story_check.py`, the INFO and the plugin that wins it). Bob judges each, and adds the ones that check out to `quest_truth.json`.

## The College of Winterhold: Faralda (was Nirya)

First Lessons (MG01) runs from the start of the game at its start-up stage 0, with nothing in the journal. With Improved College Entry (`CollegeEntry.esp`), Faralda's lines at the bridge take it straight past stage 10 to 20:

- **Faralda**, the alias MG01FaraldaAlias (her editor placement is the Hall of Countenance; the mod has her greet you at the bridge): "Okay, I can do that." Reply: "I look forward to it." Sets stage 20 while First Lessons is before stage 20. INFO Skyrim.esm:021E5D (Skyrim.esm).
- **Faralda**: "Okay, this is for the spell. (Give 30 gold)". Sets stage 20 with 30 gold, for the Magelight test. INFO Skyrim.esm:01F814 (Skyrim.esm). Her same line for the other spells sets stages 21 to 24 (INFOs 01F815 to 01F818, 01F818 from CollegeEntry.esp); those are past the next step, so the check doesn't count them as the start.
- **Nirya**, the backup gatekeeper alias MG01NiryaAlias: "What are you doing out here?" Sets stage 10 while First Lessons is before stage 30. INFO Skyrim.esm:0DF034 (Skyrim.esm).

Nothing else in the records or scripts sets First Lessons' stage 10. Since WO-121a Part 2 the start check counts Faralda's talk as the start: First Lessons runs from the start of the game at stage 0, and Nirya's line, still offered until stage 30, is a catch-up. Faralda's lines come first, and the page names Faralda. The answer key passes First Lessons.

## The Wolf Queen Awakened: Elisif's talk or Falk's?

The line opens with The Man Who Cried Wolf (MS06Start), which runs from the start of the game.

- **Elisif the Fair**, Blue Palace: "Do you need someone to check out Wolfskull Cave?" Reply: "Oh, I thought that matter was handled. I think Falk is taking care of that." Sets MS06Start's stage 15, its first objective: "Ask Falk Firebeard about Wolfskull Cave". It needs MS06Start at stage 10 to 49. INFO Skyrim.esm:0D8E97 (Skyrim.esm).
- **Falk Firebeard**: sets MS06Start's stage 50 (INFO Skyrim.esm:026C49), the step after.

The Wolf Queen Awakened (MS06), the line's second quest, is the one UESP describes:

- It's started by a story manager node, USKPMS06FixNode (Unofficial Skyrim Special Edition Patch.esp:01D104), once The Man Who Cried Wolf's stage 250 is done and you're level 10 or higher (`MS06MinLevel`): that's Falk's letter.
- **Falk Firebeard**: "I'll talk to Styrr. We'll figure something out." Sets MS06's stage 50, its first objective, "Speak to Styrr", while MS06 is at stage 10. INFO Skyrim.esm:09E00B (Skyrim.esm).

So both readings hold, for different quests. Elisif's talk sets the first stage of the line's opening quest; Falk's talk, after his letter, sets the first stage of The Wolf Queen Awakened itself. What sets MS06Start's stage 10, which Elisif's line needs, isn't among the calls the check traced.

## Infiltration: Brurid (was Stalleo)

- **Brurid**, Treva's Watch: "I'll get rid of him for you." Reply: "Excellent. Return to me once it is done and I shall reward you." Sets Infiltration's (dunTrevasWatchQST) stage 5, its first objective: "Join with Stalleo or deal with him". It needs Infiltration before stage 15, with Brurid alive. ## Seeking A Cure: Urag gro-Shub (was Falion)

Seeking A Cure (VC01) runs from the start of the game. With Rising at Dawn - Quest Overhaul (`RisingAtDawnQuestOverhaul.esp`), its first stages are talk with Urag in the Arcanaeum:

- **Urag gro-Shub**, The Arcanaeum: "(Lie) I'm just perusing the subject. Do you have anything available?" Reply: "Alright, fine. Hold on, let me check the register." Sets stage 1. INFO RisingAtDawnQuestOverhaul.esp:00F5EE.
- **Urag gro-Shub**: "I would greatly appreciate it." Reply: "Alright, give me a minute." Sets stage 3. INFO RisingAtDawnQuestOverhaul.esp:011DAC. Further lines set stage 3 (00F5FD) and stage 5 (011DA3).

The first objective, "Speak to Urag", shows at stage 7, which none of these lines sets: Urag's talk comes before the journal's first step. For Bob: Urag is the person, but what sets stage 7 isn't among the calls the check traced.

## A Forgotten Blade: Fultheim (was Delphine)

- **Fultheim**, Nightgate Inn: "I'm the one the Blades have been searching for. I'm Dragonborn." Reply: "Oh, you're the great Dragonborn, are you? Tell you what, oh mighty hero..." ## Deluge of Deceit: Wujeeta (was Frissa Black-Briar)

Deluge of Deceit (EVGSirenrootQuest, evgSIRENROOT.esm) runs from the start of the game. Two lines set its stage 1, the first objective, "Accept the job from the Black-Briars":

- **Wujeeta**, Haelga's Bunkhouse: "Find anything interesting recently?" Reply: "I don't know if it's my mind playing tricks on me, but there's this plant at the bottom of the lake that.. 'sings' to me". INFO evgSIRENROOT.esm:026BC9. Its conditions ask for Deluge of Deceit at stage 20, which looks odd for a line that sets stage 1. It may read stage 20 of another quest under the same name; worth Bob's look in the plugin.
- A Black-Briar line the records don't name the speaker of: "Regardless, here. As I said, it's diving work. Do take a look. Feel free to ask questions." INFO evgSIRENROOT.esm:0A25DD. The objective names the Black-Briars, so this is likely Frissa's or Maven's.

## Gore: Eden (was Gore)

- **Eden**, Dead Man's Drink (Falkreath): "Anything interesting going on around here?" Reply: "Lots of Imperial Patrols passing by the tower outside of town... might go have a look when you get a chance." Starts Raven's Flight (HD1GoreMeet), whose first objective is "Head to Peak's Shade Tower outside of Falkreath.", while Raven's Flight isn't completed. INFO GORE.esp:4B0CAE. Gore is who you find at the tower.

## Journey to Baan Malur: Adimmisaru (was Varanie)

- **Adimmisaru**, Arena Market: "The Altmer? How can you be sure she's Thalmor?" Reply: "Oh they're all the same. Go on, ask her. She'd have the gall to admit it too, I'll bet!" Sets The Amulet of Old's (DefunctQuest02) stage 10, its first objective: "Solve the dispute." INFO Journey to Baan Malur.esp:03DDAE. Varanie is the Altmer the dispute is with. Whether The Amulet of Old is the line's real opening quest is the curation's call (Part 1's rule: the one the player starts first).
