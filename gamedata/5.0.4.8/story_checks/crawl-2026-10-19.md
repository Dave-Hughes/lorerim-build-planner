## Line by line

**The Companions.** Take Up Arms runs from the start of the game and its first objective is "Speak
to Kodlak Whitemane", placed in Jorrvaskr. Read further, past the first objective: Kodlak's "I would
like to join the Companions" line (Companions at Mirmulnir.esp wins it) is offered only while he is in
Jorrvaskr near his seat with Vilkas nearby, with no skill test. The other Companions only point you
to him when one combat skill is 30 or more, or health or stamina is 150 or more (Narrative Gameplay
Consistent Dialogue Tweaks); that gates their hint, not joining. Worth a line on the page: "Kodlak
takes you on in Jorrvaskr when Vilkas is at hand."

**The College of Winterhold.** First Lessons runs from the start; the objective is "Join the College
of Winterhold". Improved College Entry (CollegeEntry.esp) moves Faralda's greeting back to the bridge,
lets you ask to join straight away, and stops her selling the test spell: the court wizards sell it
(`MGI_FaraldaSellsSpells` is 0). The Shout after The Way of the Voice is its change too. The page's
line holds; add "Faralda doesn't sell the spell; a court wizard does." Tool gaps: it named Nirya (the
backup gatekeeper alias) as the one who sets the first stage and placed Faralda at her dormitory,
not the bridge.

**The Thieves Guild.** A Chance Arrangement runs from the start; Brynjolf's lines set stage 5 and
the objective is "Meet Brynjolf during daytime". No patch adds a condition beyond the base game's.
Holds.

**The Dark Brotherhood.** Innocence Lost runs from the start (Innocence Lost - Quest Expansion wins
the record); the objective is "Talk to Aventus Aretino", placed in the Aretino Residence, its door
unlocked. With Friends Like These is shown started by Astrid's dialogue in the Abandoned Shack; the
tool doesn't trace what moves you there (the sleep after Grelod), so that half stays "not checked
yet" on a story page. The 11 unread quests are radiant contracts from Additional Contracts and
Listen, with no traced starter; they aren't story quests.

**Dawnguard.** The first objective is "Speak with the leader of the Dawnguard" at Fort Dawnguard,
and the level gate is `DLC1VQMinLevel` 30 from LoreRim - Global Modifiers.esp (confirmed on 18 Oct
when the Timing is Everything reading was corrected). The tool stops at `DLC1VQ00ChangeLocation`
("started by nothing"): the story manager node that starts Durak's approach isn't linked, and The
Choice Is Yours overrides that quest, which hasn't been read. Agmaer's line at the fort comes out
as "speaker not named". The page's "Guards' rumours need Laid to Rest" isn't checked by the tool
either. So: the level holds, the walk-in holds, Durak stays "not checked yet".

## What this says about the quest content

The five big lines' start instructions hold. The failures found so far (Gravewind, the delayed
Daedric starts, Vigilant) were all mod-added lines or LoreRim's own start-moving patches, which is
where the risk sits. The 152-line pages still show mod-page and field-guide text for the rest; a
line earns "game files" only when it has been through this check.
