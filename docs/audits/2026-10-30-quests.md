# Quest page accuracy audit: quests_base and quests_mod

Auditor: Rex (reviewer). Date: 2026-10-30. Sample: `sample.json`, seed 20261030, parts `quests_base` (40) and `quests_mod` (15).
Site read: staging preview, https://lorerim-build-planner-git-staging-daves-projects-a9b7d176.vercel.app, rendered with Playwright, every `<details>` opened, `main` text captured. Page URLs come from `questHref()` in `src/lib/quests/slugs.ts`; all 55 returned HTTP 200.

## Read this first: what was and was not checked

- **UESP blocked most fetches.** The first dozen-odd reads worked. After that, en.uesp.net returned `client_error` to WebFetch on almost every request, including retries after 2, 3 and 5 minute waits. I did not try to get around the block (a plain curl got a 403 bot check, and I stopped there).
- **16 of the 40 base quests were read against UESP.** Those 16 get full field-by-field verdicts and they are the only source of the error rates below.
- **24 base quests could not be read on UESP.** For those I give only page-internal and plausibility findings. No verdict on them is presented as checked.
- **Caveat on the rates:** the 16 are the quests whose UESP page loaded, not a random draw from the 40. They are not hand-picked by me, but the rates carry that bias.
- **All 15 mod quests** were judged only for internal consistency and plausibility. Every one of them needs Dave's PC (the game records) for a real check.

Verdict key: **R** right; **W** wrong; **D** differs from UESP, may be LoreRim; **U** unknown (the page says it doesn't know, either "not known yet" or the field is listed under Not sure yet); **M** missing (a fact a player needs that the page leaves out and does not flag); **V** unverifiable (UESP doesn't say either).

Rule used for flags: a field the page flags and leaves blank is U. A field the page flags but still fills with a wrong value is W.

What the numbers say:
- The **journal-derived fields** (first step, steps, places) were clean on every checked quest. Those come straight from the records.
- The **gate** and **reward** fields are where the trouble is. Error plus unknown comes to about half of all gate and reward cells. On 6 of 16 quests a real prerequisite was absent from the page (a skill level, a prior quest, the Amulet of Mara, level 20).
- Besides the 10 fields, the **"Level and danger" block** was wrong or implausible on 10 of 55 pages read (see family F1). It is not in the table because I could not check it against UESP, but it fails internal checks.

## Top fault families (would recur across the dataset)

**F1. Danger block built from a raw actor census.** It labels friendly hubs and spirit dungeons as bandit lairs.
- The Dark Brotherhood Sanctuary shows "bandits from level 8, up to 78; the boss Penitus Oculatus Agent, level 19" on every DB page in the sample: Honor Thy Family, Kill Beitild, Kill Agnis, Kill Ennodius Papius, Recipe for Disaster. That is the player's own guild home.
- Twilight Sepulcher shows "bandits from level 8, up to 45; the boss level 3". The page's own journal text says the danger is hostile Nightingale guardian spirits.
- Meridia's Sanctum (the Order's home base) shows necromancers from level 1 to 80.
- **Likely cause:** `places.json` `actors.livesHere` defaults to "Bandit" when NPC classes aren't recognised. It also counts actors that only appear in later quests (Penitus Oculatus agents from the Sanctuary's destruction), and it uses a nameless leveled boss's level as-is. The source data shows `"livesHere": "Bandit", "boss": {"name": "Penitus Oculatus Agent"...}` on the Sanctuary.
- **Fix:** don't show a danger block for a place that is the quest-giver's hub, and drop "Bandit" as a fallback class.

**F2. Gates and prerequisites the records don't spell out are dropped, while the chip still says "Any level".**
- Restoration Ritual Spell is missing Restoration 90 and Good Intentions.
- The Whispering Door says "Any level"; UESP says level 20 and Dragon Rising.
- The Words of Power is missing The Horn of Jurgen Windcaller.
- The Bonds of Matrimony is missing that you must wear an Amulet of Mara to propose.
- Black Book: The Winds of Change is missing "Start The Final Descent".
- Wind and Sand is missing Old Friends, Experimental Subject and Destruction 40.
- The Ultimate Feast is missing Spirits Away.
- **LoreRim's own category text contradicts the pages:** the Daedric Princes shelf says "LoreRim holds almost all of them back until level 20, and Hircine until 25", yet Ill Met By Moonlight, The Whispering Door and both Boethiah pages say "Any level".
- **Likely cause:** the gate is read only from dialogue conditions on the start line. Story-manager, skill and global conditions come out as "a condition the planner can't put in words yet", and the "Any level" chip is the default when nothing is found.
- **Fix:** the chip should say "Not known" instead of "Any level" whenever `start.needs` contains an unreadable condition or `What you need` is flagged. The shelf-level rule (Daedric = 20, Hircine = 25) should feed the chip.

**F3. Rewards that aren't in the quest record show as blank or wrong.**
- Gold amounts on DB contracts, the Burglary Job and Family Heirloom are left out; UESP gives the leveled tables.
- The Black Book's actual reward (a choice of Companion's, Lover's or Scholar's Insight) is missing.
- The Man Who Cried Wolf shows "A medium leveled quest reward" while the data says `rewards: {gold: "medium"}`. The renderer drops the word "gold".
- The Bonds of Matrimony lists the **Amulet of Mara**, the prerequisite item, as the reward.
- **Likely cause:** rewards come only from the quest's own reward fields and script properties. Script-given gold and choice rewards aren't captured, and the reader mistook an item the quest references for an item it gives.

**F4. Cut or disabled quests presented as playable.**
- Boethiah's Bidding (DA02KillObj): UESP's `Skyrim:Boethiah's_Bidding` is a redirect into "Skyrim:Unfinished Quests" (the search result titles it that way; the body could not be fetched).
- The page says "Starts by itself… Boethiah sends you to kill the Jarl of Solitude".
- The planner's own data has no start record (`"checked": "not yet"`, "No start record. It's tied to Boethiah's Calling through its log text"). The reader still invented a giver ("Boethiah, after you earn the Ebony Mail").
- The data `type` is also "Companions", which is wrong.
- The pages that are correctly marked "Cut from the game" (Kill the Bandit Leader, Delivery) show the right handling exists. It just isn't applied when the start is simply absent.
- **Likely cause:** when no start record exists, the read layer fills `starts` from journal text instead of marking it unreachable.

**F5. The start falls back to the place when the giver comes from a story-manager event.**
- The Words of Power: the page says it "fires on its own when you enter High Hrothgar". UESP says Arngeir gives it when you ask to learn more, after The Horn of Jurgen Windcaller.
- Imitation Amnesty and Silver Lining show the placeholder "Talk to someone to start it" (Delvin Mallory hands these out in vanilla, unverified here because UESP was blocked).
- **Likely cause:** SM-node starts without a speaker are rendered as "fires on entry" or as a generic placeholder.

**F6. Free-text fields split or cut off in the read layer.**
- Bloodline shows "Castle Volkiharnearest city Solitude)". The data has `"hold": "nearest city Solitude)"`, so a parenthetical was split into the hold field.
- Hendraheim shows Reburrus Quintilius "spends 8 am to 8 pm around the upper part of Markarth and." (cut off mid-sentence).
- Recipe for Disaster says "Nightgate Inn (Balagog is placed in its cellar)", which is editor-placement wording leaking into player text.
- Bloodline also says both "Castle Volkihar" and "Volkihar Keep".
- **Likely cause:** hand or LLM-written `read.*` text is not validated (no balanced-paren check, no sentence-end check).

**F7. Duplicate or empty person entries.**
- Our Lady links "Alara" to /people/alara and "High Priestess Alara" to /people/high-priestess-alara, which are two person pages for one NPC.
- Ill Met By Moonlight lists Mathies twice.
- Unusual Imports gives Signy no role.
- **Likely cause:** person names are not resolved to a single form id before the page is built.

**F8. Missives pages are a generic template, and the line page doesn't link them.**
- All 7 Missives pages say "each is a short delivery". That is wrong for "Kill Some Things", "Kill a Dragon" and "Gather Rare Ore".
- They say "notice board in any hold's capital" for Alik'r Basin (Hammerfell) and Solstheim jobs.
- They show "Collect bounty from the jarl" for Solstheim, and the source does not say Solstheim has a jarl.
- The header says "How it starts: Not known yet", yet Not sure yet says "Nothing is flagged".
- The Missives line page (/quests/missives) says "384 quests" but links to none of them. These pages can only be reached through search.
- **Likely cause:** "planner's older rules" pages were never re-read, and the line template doesn't list its children.

**F9. "Ways to finish" built only from fail stages.**
- Back-Stabbing Rodents lists one way to finish: "Turn down Drevis's class". The success path is not given as an ending.
- **Likely cause:** ending detection keys on stages that set fail or complete flags and skips the normal completion.
