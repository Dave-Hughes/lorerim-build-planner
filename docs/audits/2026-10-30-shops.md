## Wrong (12)

**Kidmer orphans shown as market stalls.** They use Sofie's flower vendor list (`BYOHUrchin_SofieVendorList`), their chest is in WindhelmWorld, and the filter only skips factions whose names start with "BYOH". Elandra (Kidmer `004904`) also has no market in Falkreath, and Terlan is not a merchant on UESP.
- Elandra (Falkreath)
- Brundil (Markarth)
- Urshug (Winterhold, which has no market)
- Terlan (Windhelm)

**Shown as a seller, but isn't one.** Adara (Markarth, jewelry) is Kerah's child. UESP says she has no trade dialogue, so the stall is Kerah's. Her link goes to the Stonehills Adara.

**Wrong keeper.** The keeper rule picks whoever sleeps there, not the faction's owner.
- The Hag's Cure shows Muiri. Bothela is the keeper; Muiri is her assistant.
- The Solitude Blacksmith shows Heimvar. Beirand is the keeper; Heimvar is his apprentice.

**One vendor faction split into two shops.** Each pair below shares one faction (`ServicesSolitudeEastEmpireCompany` for the first) and shows as two shops.
- Solitude: Aquillius Aeresius and Vittoria Vici, each listed as "general goods". UESP calls both non-traders; an in-game check would settle it.
- Windhelm: Hermir and Oengul (UESP: one shop, and Hermir trades only if Oengul dies).
- Dawnstar: Seren and Rustleif.

**Wrong kind.** The kind comes from the first matching keyword.
- The Drunken Huntsman (Whiterun) shows "weapons". Elrindir is a Fletcher and buys armor, arrows, food, tools and weapons.

## Missing (10)

**Seller with no town placement.** Madesi's jewelry stall in Riften's market is in `ServicesRiftenPlazaMadesi` (RiftenWorld), but he has no town placement, so he is dropped.

**Merged into one shop because shops are keyed by building, or by cell and kind.**
- The Ragged Flagon, Riften: Arnskar Ember-Master, Syndus and Vanryth Gatharian (blacksmiths) and Herluin Lothaire (apothecary) all fold into Vekel's.
- The Hall of Countenance, Winterhold: Drevis Neloren, Faralda and Phinis Gestor (spells) fold into Colette's.
- Understone Keep, Markarth: Moth gro-Bagol (castle smith) folds into Ghorza's.

**Left out on purpose.** Sofie (flowers) at the Windhelm gate was skipped as an urchin, while the orphans who use the same list are shown. Use one rule for both.
