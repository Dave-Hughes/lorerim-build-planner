## 3. What we learned

**The approach works.** In the bake-off, agents reading the records got 72 of 100 fields right, where the old rules got 48 of 100. Across the whole run, measured against UESP on the 385 checked quests UESP covers: who starts it agrees on 220 of 251 (88%), the first step on 350 of 384 (91%). Most of the misses are radiant jobs whose giver LoreRim's records don't name (Dawnguard's jobs for Gunmar, Florentius and Feran Sadri, the Thieves Guild city jobs from Delvin); UESP fills those.

**The checker is what makes it trustworthy.** Almost every batch had real fixes: a reader turning a guess into a fact (Ancient Technology's crossbows were only labels; "fistfight" in no record), a remembered Skyrim detail that isn't in LoreRim ("cornerclub", Morihaus, "Iddra runs the Braidwood Inn"), or a wrong giver (Rapture of the Deep is Tolfdir's, not Arniel's). One rule we had to add mid-run: keep a reward the quest's own script names, marked unsure, rather than deleting it.

**LoreRim really is different from vanilla.** Readers found things UESP would get wrong here: Winterhold's jarl sits in Stonecold Fortress, Falkreath's in Hakkvild's High Hall; Save the Icerunner keeps Jaree-Ra at the Winking Skeever (what Dave saw); ACDB's contracts target essential people (Elenwen, Erikur, Rolff) and may not be finishable.

**People's schedules change the answer.** Once the PC's schedule dump landed (WO-156), checkers moved dozens of givers from "where they're placed" to where they actually are by the hour: Sergius is in the Hall of the Elements afternoons, not the Hall of Countenance where he sleeps; Ysolda, Igna, Degaine, Yngvar the same.

**Real disagreements to check in game** (the reads and UESP differ): who starts Mourning Never Comes (read: Astrid; UESP's key: Nazir), Recipe for Disaster (Festus Krex / Astrid), Where You Hang Your Enemy's Head (Delvin Mallory / Nazir), Extracting an Argonian (Derkeethus / Sondas Drenim), The Unquiet Dead (Proventus / Mralki), Raven's Flight (Gore / Eden); and Research Thief, which UESP calls cut but LoreRim's records have dialogue for.

**What it cost, and how to run it next time.** About 175 agent runs at 150,000 to 270,000 tokens each, all on the top model, 20 at a time: about 35 million tokens in an hour and a half, which took Dave's weekly usage to 81%. Next time: the cheaper model, at most 5 at a time, and a cost estimate from the first batch before the rest go.

## 4. The quest pages now (read live on staging, 19:35)

Read: Lights Out!, The Golden Claw, Running Wild, the Thieves Guild line, More to do in Whiterun.

**What works.** The page is now the shape we agreed: can I do it, what you need first, who and where (with hours), where it takes you with how dangerous each place is, the choices in plain words, what you get, the steps grouped by branch, how sure. Lights Out! is right and readable; The Golden Claw has all three ways in (Lucan, the locals, overhearing the bandits).

**What's wrong**
1. **The "why we're unsure" notes read like an agent's working notes, not a page.** "These files don't show what starts the quest", "the page can say 'probably Joslin'", "Erland has no daytime package". A player needs one plain line ("Not confirmed: which ending gives the book"), not the reader's reasoning.
2. **A player's report doesn't override the read everywhere.** Running Wild shows Dave's in-game find (the note on the board) as how to start it, but "Can I do it?" still says "Starts by itself" and the unsure note still guesses Joslin. A field a player confirmed should drop the read's doubt about it.
3. **The jobs page groups by the wrong thing.** "More to do in Whiterun" groups jobs under headings like "outside near Dragonsreach by day (9 am to 8 pm); at the Drunken Huntsman 8 to 10 pm..." and "Where to pick it up isn't read yet (5 jobs)". It's grouping by the giver's whole "where" text, not by the place.
4. **Bad place data still shows.** Igna's Basement: "warlocks from level 1, up to 50; the boss level 1". That's the places index, not the quest read.
5. **Small things.** "in inside Bleak Falls Barrow"; the Thieves Guild line says "25 quests pay 100" of 30 with no word on the other five; the line page's "What the whole line gives" lists every reward of every quest, down to radiant jobs' gold, because the line reads aren't done.

- Hours: inline with the person ("Lucan, in the Riverwood Trader, 7 am to 8 pm") or a separate "When" line?
