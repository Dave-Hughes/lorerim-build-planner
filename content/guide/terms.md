## LoreRim {#lorerim}
A modlist: about 3,900 mods for Skyrim Anniversary Edition, chosen and set up to work together. This guide covers version 5.0.4.8.

## Modlist {#modlist}
- aka: mod list, the list
A set of mods installed together and tested as one game. You install the whole list at once and don't add or remove mods yourself.

## Mod {#mod}
- aka: mods
A change to the game made by players: new items, new rules, new places or a new interface.

## Wabbajack {#wabbajack}
The free installer that downloads every mod in LoreRim and sets them up for you.

## Nexus Mods {#nexus}
- aka: Nexus
The website where most Skyrim mods are published. The mod names in Related Mods link to their Nexus pages.

## Mod Organizer 2 {#mo2}
- aka: MO2
The program LoreRim installs and that you start the game from. Its left pane lists the mods, its right pane the plugins, and the list at the top picks the profile.
- see: /guide/what-lorerim-changes#profiles

## Plugin {#plugin}
- aka: plugins
A data file (.esp, .esm or .esl) that adds or changes things in the game. When two plugins change the same thing, the one that loads later wins.

## Profile {#profile}
- aka: profiles
A saved setup in MO2: which mods are on, and the game's settings. LoreRim has three: Default, Ultra and Extreme.
- see: /guide/what-lorerim-changes#profiles

## Requiem {#requiem}
- nexus: 60888
The large mod LoreRim's gameplay is built on. It changes levels, skills, perks, combat, magic and prices.
- see: /guide/what-lorerim-changes#requiem

## MCM {#mcm}
- aka: Mod Configuration Menu
The settings menu for mods, opened from the pause menu (Esc > Mod Configuration). Each mod with settings has its own page.
- see: /guide/keys-and-menus#mcm

## ini file {#ini}
- aka: ini
A plain text settings file. Open it with Notepad, change the value after the = sign, and save.

## SKSE {#skse}
- aka: Skyrim Script Extender
A tool many mods need. LoreRim starts the game through it, so you don't need to do anything with it.

## SKSE menu {#skse-menu}
- aka: F6 menu
A settings menu opened with F6, for mods that have no MCM page.
- see: /guide/keys-and-menus#f6

## ENB {#enb}
A tool that changes the game's lighting and colours. LoreRim includes several ENB presets; you pick one in MO2.

## Creation Club {#creation-club}
Bethesda's own add-ons, included with Skyrim Anniversary Edition. LoreRim uses many of them, such as Survival Mode, fishing and camping.

## LoreRim Discord {#discord}
- aka: Discord, the Discord
The Modding Bungalo, the Discord server where LoreRim's team and players answer questions. The site footer links to it.

## Rule 11 {#rule-11}
The LoreRim Discord's support rule: if you change the modlist, the staff can't help with problems that follow.
- see: /guide/what-lorerim-changes#changing

## Console {#console}
A command line for fixes, opened with the ~ key. Type a command and press Enter.

## RaceMenu {#racemenu}
The character creation screen, where you choose your race and appearance.

## Active Effects {#active-effects}
A list in the Magic menu (Magic > Active Effects) of everything currently affecting you: needs, diseases, buffs, traits and your god's rules.

## HUD {#hud}
What the game draws over the world while you play: health bars, the compass, messages.
- see: /guide/keys-and-menus#hud

## Widgets {#widgets}
- aka: widget, STB Widgets
Small readouts on the HUD for your level, XP, carry weight, resistances and the time. C hides and shows them.
- see: /guide/keys-and-menus#hud

## Wheeler {#wheeler}
- nexus: 97345
A round quick menu, opened with G, for equipping weapons, spells, shouts and powers.
- see: /guide/keys-and-menus#wheeler

## IED {#ied}
- aka: Immersive Equipment Display, equipment display
- nexus: 62001
The mod that shows weapons and gear hanging on your character's belt and back. Backspace opens its menu.
- see: /guide/keys-and-menus#ied

## dMenu {#dmenu}
A settings menu for some mods, opened with Page Down.

## XP {#xp}
- aka: experience
Points that fill your level bar. In LoreRim they come from quests, discovering places, clearing dungeons and kills.
- see: /guide/first-days#xp

## Level-up {#level-up}
- aka: level up, leveling up
What happens when your XP bar is full and you sleep: you gain a level and get skill points and a perk point.
- see: /guide/first-days#level-up

## Skill {#skill}
- aka: skills
One of 18 abilities, such as One-Handed or Alchemy, each from 0 to 100. Higher skills make you better at it and unlock perks.

## Skill points {#skill-points}
Points you get at each level-up (30) and spend by hand to raise skills.
- see: /guide/first-days#level-up

## Base skill {#base-skill}
Your skill without bonuses from gear or potions. Perks and Smithing books check this number.

## Perk {#perk}
- aka: perks
- icon: mark-perk-points
A permanent ability bought with a perk point in a skill's tree, such as cheaper spells or a dodge.
- see: /guide/first-days#perks

## Perk point {#perk-point}
- aka: perk points
- icon: mark-perk-points
What you spend to take a perk. You get 1 per level.

## Perk tree {#perk-tree}
- aka: perk trees, tree
The perks of one skill, shown as stars in the skill menu. Most perks need the one before them.

## Feat {#feat}
- aka: feats
A perk that needs a character level as well as, or instead of, a skill level.

## Destiny {#destiny}
- icon: destiny
A separate perk tree with its own 7 points, for general bonuses such as regeneration and damage.
- see: /guide/first-days#perks

## Major skill {#major-skill}
- aka: major skills, major
A skill you pick at the start for +10.

## Minor skill {#minor-skill}
- aka: minor skills, minor
A skill you pick at the start for +5.

## Birthsign {#birthsign}
- aka: birthsigns
A permanent bonus you choose at the start, named after a constellation.
- see: /guide/character#birthsign

## Standing stone {#standing-stone}
- aka: standing stones, stone, stones
A carved stone in the world that gives a small bonus when you touch it. You can hold one at a time.
- see: /guide/character#birthsign

## Trait {#trait}
- aka: traits
A permanent ability with a benefit and a drawback, chosen at the start. You can take up to 2.
- see: /guide/character#traits

## Naaktiid {#naaktiid}
- icon: mode-naaktiid
The easier game mode ("New to LoreRim"). It turns on Requiem Lite.
- see: /guide/character#mode

## Konahrik {#konahrik}
- icon: mode-konahrik
The standard game mode ("Classic LoreRim Experience"), with Requiem's full rules.
- see: /guide/character#mode

## Requiem Lite {#requiem-lite}
- nexus: 120272
Easier rules for Requiem: more health, magicka and stamina, faster regeneration, and less damage at low levels. Naaktiid turns it on.
- see: /guide/character#mode

## Health {#health}
- icon: attr-health
Your life. At 0 you fall and the respawn rules apply.

## Magicka {#magicka}
- icon: attr-magicka
What spells cost. It refills slowly over time.

## Stamina {#stamina}
- icon: attr-stamina
What attacks, blocks, sprinting and dodging cost. It refills over time.
- see: /guide/fighting#stamina

## One-Handed {#one-handed}
- icon: one-handed
The skill for swords, axes, maces and daggers held in one hand.

## Two-Handed {#two-handed}
- icon: two-handed
The skill for greatswords, battleaxes, warhammers, quarterstaves and other two-handed weapons.

## Marksman {#marksman}
- aka: Archery
- icon: marksman
LoreRim's name for Archery: bows and crossbows.

## Block {#block-skill}
- icon: block
The skill for blocking with a shield or weapon.

## Heavy Armor {#heavy-armor}
- icon: heavy-armor
The skill for wearing heavy armor such as iron, steel and plate.

## Evasion {#evasion}
- aka: Light Armor
- icon: evasion
LoreRim's name for Light Armor. Its tree also holds the no-armor perks and the Dodge perk.

## Smithing {#smithing}
- icon: smithing
The skill for making and improving weapons and armor.

## Alchemy {#alchemy}
- icon: alchemy
The skill for making potions and poisons.

## Enchanting {#enchanting}
- icon: enchanting
The skill for putting magic effects on weapons, armor and jewelry.

## Sneak {#sneak}
- icon: sneak
The skill for staying unseen and for sneak attacks.

## Finesse {#finesse}
- aka: Pickpocket
- icon: finesse
LoreRim's name for Pickpocket. Its tree also holds trap perks.

## Wayfarer {#wayfarer}
- aka: Lockpicking
- icon: wayfarer
LoreRim's name for Lockpicking. Its tree also holds perks for followers, animal taming and carry weight.

## Speech {#speech}
- aka: Speechcraft
- icon: speech
The skill for prices, persuasion and shouts. It also sets how many followers you can have.

## Alteration {#alteration}
- icon: alteration
The magic school for armor spells, light, telekinesis and paralysis.

## Conjuration {#conjuration}
- icon: conjuration
The magic school for summoning creatures and weapons and raising the dead.

## Destruction {#destruction}
- icon: destruction
The magic school for damaging spells: fire, frost, shock and others.

## Illusion {#illusion}
- icon: illusion
The magic school for spells that affect minds: fear, calm, frenzy, invisibility.

## Restoration {#restoration}
- icon: restoration
The magic school for healing, wards and spells against the undead.

## Altmer {#altmer}
- aka: High Elf, High Elves
- icon: race-altmer
High Elves. They start with the most Magicka.

## Argonian {#argonian}
- aka: Argonians
- icon: race-argonian
The lizard people of Black Marsh.

## Bosmer {#bosmer}

- icon: race-bosmer
Wood Elves, known as archers.

## Breton {#breton}
- aka: Bretons
- icon: race-breton
People of High Rock, part elf, with a talent for magic.

## Dunmer {#dunmer}
- aka: Dark Elf, Dark Elves
- icon: race-dunmer
Dark Elves of Morrowind.

## Imperial {#imperial}
- aka: Imperials
- icon: race-imperial
People of Cyrodiil, the heart of the Empire.

## Khajiit {#khajiit}
- icon: race-khajiit
The cat people of Elsweyr.

## Nord {#nord}
- aka: Nords
- icon: race-nord
The people of Skyrim.

## Orsimer {#orsimer}
- aka: Orc, Orcs
- icon: race-orsimer
Orcs.

## Redguard {#redguard}
- aka: Redguards
- icon: race-redguard
People of Hammerfell, known as warriors.

## Lord birthsign {#sign-lord}
- aka: Lord
- icon: sign-lord
A birthsign: +150 armor, 15% magic resistance and Health regeneration, but 30% weakness to fire.

## Tower birthsign {#sign-tower}
- aka: Tower
- icon: sign-tower
A birthsign that helps with locks, carry weight and gold.

## Atronach birthsign {#sign-atronach}
- aka: Atronach
- icon: sign-atronach
A birthsign: +300 Magicka, but no natural Magicka regeneration.

## Ritual birthsign {#sign-ritual}
- aka: Ritual
- icon: sign-ritual
A birthsign that strengthens soul gems and blessings.

## Nine Divines {#divines}
- aka: Divines, the Nine Divines
The gods of the Empire: Akatosh, Arkay, Dibella, Julianos, Kynareth, Mara, Stendarr, Talos and Zenithar. Anyone may follow them.

## Daedric Prince {#daedric-prince}
- aka: Daedric Princes, Daedra
A powerful being of Oblivion with its own realm and followers, such as Azura or Molag Bal. Each has a quest.

## Daedric quest {#daedric-quest}
- aka: Daedric quests
A quest given by a Daedric Prince. Most wait for level 20 in LoreRim.

## Wintersun {#wintersun}
- nexus: 22506
The mod that adds religion: following a god, praying and favor.
- see: /guide/followers-gods-curses#gods

## Favor {#favor}
Your standing with the god you follow, from 0 to 200%. At 100% you become a Devotee; at 0% the god abandons you.
- see: /guide/followers-gods-curses#gods

## Tenets {#tenets}
- aka: tenet
Your god's rules. They are listed in Active Effects.

## Devotee {#devotee}
What you become at 100% favor. It gives your god's second, stronger gift.

## Shrine {#shrine}
- aka: shrines
A god's altar. Activate it to join that god or to get its 24-hour blessing.

## Arkay {#arkay}
- icon: deity-arkay
One of the Nine Divines, the god of life and death.

## Riddle'Thar {#riddle-thar}
- icon: deity-riddle-thar
A Khajiit god. Only Khajiit may follow him.

## Stendarr {#stendarr}
- icon: deity-stendarr
One of the Nine Divines, the god of mercy, who hates undead and Daedra.

## Kynareth {#kynareth}
- icon: deity-kynareth
One of the Nine Divines, the goddess of the sky and nature.

## Dibella {#dibella}
- icon: deity-dibella
One of the Nine Divines, the goddess of beauty.

## Azura {#azura}
- icon: deity-azura
A Daedric Prince of dawn and dusk.

## Nocturnal {#nocturnal}
- icon: deity-nocturnal
A Daedric Prince of night and thieves.

## Mannimarco {#mannimarco}
- icon: deity-mannimarco
A god of necromancers.

## Hircine {#hircine}
- icon: deity-hircine
A Daedric Prince of the hunt and werewolves.

## Sotha Sil {#sotha-sil}
- icon: deity-sotha-sil
One of the three Tribunal gods of the Dunmer.

## Vivec {#vivec}
- icon: deity-vivec
One of the three Tribunal gods of the Dunmer.

## Main quest {#main-quest}
The Dragonborn story that begins at Helgen. In LoreRim it only starts when you choose to start it.
- see: /guide/character#start

## Side quest {#side-quest}
- aka: side quests
A quest outside the main story and the guilds. Side quests pay 100 XP.

## Miscellaneous quest {#misc-quest}
- aka: miscellaneous quests, misc quest
A small errand listed under Miscellaneous in the journal. In LoreRim these pay gold but no XP.

## Missive {#missive}
- aka: missives
A job note from the board in a hold's capital. Every missive pays 100 XP and some gold.
- see: /guide/first-days#jobs

## Hold {#hold}
- aka: holds
One of Skyrim's nine regions, such as Whiterun Hold or the Reach, each ruled by a jarl from its capital.

## Jarl {#jarl}
The ruler of a hold.

## Steward {#steward}
The jarl's adviser, who pays out bounties.

## Thane {#thane}
An honorary title a jarl gives you after you help the hold's people.

## Bounty {#bounty}
- aka: bounties
1. A price on your head for crimes in a hold. 2. A paid job to kill a target, from a jarl or a missive.

## Fence {#fence}
- aka: fences
A merchant who buys stolen goods, mostly members of the Thieves Guild.

## Helgen {#helgen}
The town where every LoreRim game begins, in the inn, before the dragon attack.
- see: /guide/character#start

## Alternate Perspective {#alternate-perspective}
- nexus: 50307
The mod that lets you choose your start at the dragon statue in the Helgen inn.
- see: /guide/character#start

## Start {#start}
- aka: starts
Where your character begins and what they own, chosen at the dragon statue in the Helgen inn.
- see: /guide/character#start

## Survival Mode {#survival-mode}
A Creation Club add-on that adds hunger, fatigue and cold. LoreRim turns it on for every game and adds thirst.
- see: /guide/staying-alive#needs

## Hunger {#hunger}
- icon: need-hunger
A need that rises over time. Food lowers it.
- see: /guide/staying-alive#needs

## Thirst {#thirst}
- icon: need-thirst
A need that rises over time. Drinks lower it.
- see: /guide/staying-alive#food

## Fatigue {#fatigue}
- icon: need-fatigue
A need that rises while you are awake. Sleep lowers it.
- see: /guide/staying-alive#sleep

## Cold {#cold}
- icon: need-cold
A need that rises in cold places, at night and when wet. Warm clothes, fires and hot food lower it.
- see: /guide/staying-alive#cold

## Game time {#game-time}
- aka: game hour, game hours, game day, game days
Time in the game world. One game hour passes every 6 real minutes.

## Provisions {#provisions}
An item sold by general goods merchants. Carry one and you wake at your last bed or campfire when you die.
- see: /guide/death-and-saving#death

## Hall of the Dead {#hall-of-the-dead}
A city's temple and tomb of the god Arkay. Without Provisions, you wake in the hold's Hall of the Dead when you die.
- see: /guide/death-and-saving#death

## Respawn point {#respawn-point}
- aka: respawn
Where you wake after dying: your last bed or campfire, if you carry Provisions.
- see: /guide/death-and-saving#death

## Power attack {#power-attack}
- aka: power attacks
A strong, slow swing that costs much more stamina. Middle mouse button in LoreRim.
- see: /guide/fighting#stamina

## Poise {#poise}
A hidden bar beside health. Hits lower it, and at 0 the character staggers.
- see: /guide/fighting#block

## Stagger {#stagger}
- aka: staggers, staggered
A short stumble when poise runs out. A staggered character can't attack or block.
- see: /guide/fighting#block

## Stance {#stance}
- aka: stances
One of four fighting styles you switch with F1 to F4, each with a bonus and a drawback.
- see: /guide/fighting#stances

## Dodge {#dodge}
A quick step with a moment where you can't be hit, on Left Alt, once you have the Dodge perk.
- see: /guide/fighting#dodge

## Armor rating {#armor-rating}
- aka: armor
The number on your gear that lowers physical damage: 1% less per 10 points, up to 75%.
- see: /guide/fighting#enemies

## Armor penetration {#armor-penetration}
How much of an enemy's armor your attacks ignore.

## Critical hit {#critical}
- aka: critical, critical chance, critical damage
A hit that does extra damage by chance.

## Attack commitment {#attack-commitment}
Once a swing starts, you can't cancel it by moving, and turning is slow until it finishes.
- see: /guide/fighting#stamina

## Legendary enemy {#legendary-enemy}
- aka: legendary
A rare, stronger version of an enemy of level 20 or more, with double health.
- see: /guide/fighting#enemies

## Magic resistance {#magic-resistance}
The share of spell damage you ignore, up to 75%.

## Spell tome {#spell-tome}
- aka: tome, tomes, spell tomes
A book that teaches a spell. In LoreRim reading it starts a ritual.
- see: /guide/magic#learning

## Rite of Arcana {#rite-of-arcana}
- nexus: 168649
The ritual for learning a spell from a tome: your Magicka drains, and you learn the spell if it lasts.
- see: /guide/magic#learning

## Tier perk {#tier-perk}
- aka: tier perks
The Novice, Apprentice, Adept, Expert and Master perks in each magic school. Each makes that tier's spells cheaper and stronger.
- see: /guide/magic#tiers

## Dual casting {#dual-casting}
- aka: dual-cast, dual cast
Casting the same spell with both hands for a stronger effect. Needs the school's Empowered perk.
- see: /guide/magic#tiers

## Flesh spell {#flesh-spell}
- aka: flesh spells
An Alteration spell that raises your armor for a while: Oakflesh, Stoneflesh, Ironflesh, Ebonyflesh, Dragonhide.
- see: /guide/magic#armor-spells

## Ward {#ward}
- aka: wards
A Restoration spell you hold in one hand to block spell damage.
- see: /guide/magic#wards

## Soul gem {#soul-gem}
- aka: soul gems
A gem that holds a soul. Filled ones power enchanting and recharge enchanted weapons.

## Arcane enchanter {#arcane-enchanter}
The table where you enchant items and break enchanted items down to learn their effects.

## Court wizard {#court-wizard}
- aka: court wizards
The wizard in a jarl's palace, such as Farengar in Whiterun. Court wizards sell spell tomes.

## College of Winterhold {#college}
- aka: the College
The mages' guild, in the town of Winterhold.

## Thieves Guild {#thieves-guild}
The thieves' guild, in the Ragged Flagon under Riften.

## Companions {#companions}
The warriors' guild, at Jorrvaskr in Whiterun.

## Forge {#forge}
Where you make weapons and armor. Found at every blacksmith.

## Grindstone {#grindstone}
Where you temper weapons.

## Workbench {#workbench}
Where you temper armor.

## Smelter {#smelter}
Where you turn ore into ingots.

## Tanning rack {#tanning-rack}
Where you turn hides into leather.

## Tempering {#tempering}
- aka: temper, tempered
Improving a weapon's damage or armor's protection at a grindstone or workbench.
- see: /guide/crafting-and-money#tempering

## Craftsmanship {#craftsmanship}
The first Smithing perk, from the book The Armorer's Encyclopedia. You need it for almost all smithing.
- see: /guide/crafting-and-money#smithing

## Alchemy lab {#alchemy-lab}
- aka: lab
The table where you brew potions and poisons.

## Ingredient {#ingredient}
- aka: ingredients
A plant, animal part or other item you mix at an alchemy lab. Each has four hidden effects.

## Fast travel {#fast-travel}
Jumping to a place on the map without walking. Off by default in LoreRim.
- see: /guide/getting-around#fast-travel

## Saddlebags {#saddlebags}
Your horse's storage, opened with H while looking at it.
- see: /guide/getting-around#horses

## Lockpicking expertise {#expertise}
- aka: expertise
A number that decides which locks you can try. In Skyrim without mods, any lock could be picked with enough lockpicks; in LoreRim you need enough expertise first.
- see: /guide/getting-around#locks

## Quicksave {#quicksave}
- aka: quicksaves
A save made with F5. In LoreRim it is a full save.
- see: /guide/death-and-saving#saving

## Autosave {#autosave}
- aka: autosaves
A save the game makes on its own every 15 minutes.

## Follower {#follower}
- aka: followers
A character who travels and fights with you.
- see: /guide/followers-gods-curses#followers

## Hireling {#hireling}
A follower you pay to travel with you.

## Housecarl {#housecarl}
A follower a jarl gives you when you become thane.

## Protected {#protected}
A protected character can only be knocked down by enemies. Only your own hits can kill them.

## Essential {#essential}
An essential character can't die at all.

## Friendly fire {#friendly-fire}
Your attacks hitting your own followers.

## Vampire {#vampire}
- aka: vampires, vampirism
A curse that turns you into a vampire. LoreRim's vampires come from the mod Sacrilege, and the Vampire start begins as one.
- see: /guide/followers-gods-curses#vampires

## Sacrilege {#sacrilege}
- nexus: 42408
The mod that runs vampirism in LoreRim.

## Werewolf {#werewolf}
- aka: werewolves, lycanthropy
A curse that lets you turn into a beast. LoreRim's werewolves come from the mod Growl, and the Werewolf start begins as one.
- see: /guide/followers-gods-curses#werewolves

## Growl {#growl}
- nexus: 31245
The mod that runs werewolves in LoreRim.

## TrueHUD {#truehud}

The mod that draws your health, magicka and stamina bars and the bars over enemies' heads.

## moreHUD {#morehud}

The mod that shows an item's value and weight, and an enemy's level, when you look at them.

## QuickLoot {#quickloot}
- nexus: 120075
The small loot window that opens when you look at a container or a body.

A birthsign for fighters: more Health and weapon damage.

A birthsign for sneaking and avoiding hits.

## Mage birthsign {#sign-mage}
- icon: sign-mage

## Apprentice birthsign {#sign-apprentice}
- icon: sign-apprentice
A birthsign: cheaper spells and fast Magicka regeneration, but weaker to magic.

## Lady birthsign {#sign-lady}

A birthsign: more Health and Magicka, both regenerating faster.

## Lover birthsign {#sign-lover}

## Serpent birthsign {#sign-serpent}

## Shadow birthsign {#sign-shadow}

A birthsign for sneaking.

## Steed birthsign {#sign-steed}
- icon: sign-steed
A birthsign: faster movement, more Stamina, and resistance to slowing.

## Dragonborn {#dragonborn}
The hero of the main quest, who can absorb dragons' souls and learn shouts. In LoreRim you choose whether to play that story.

What you absorb from a dragon you kill as the Dragonborn. It unlocks shout words, and some LoreRim features cost one.

A power of the Dragonborn, used with Z, then slow to recover.

- icon: mark-power
A special ability used with Z, like the Pray power from your god.

## Trainer {#trainer}
- aka: trainers
- icon: mark-trainer
A character who raises one of your skills for gold, one point per session.
- see: /guide/first-days#level-up

## Skill book {#skill-book}
- aka: skill books
- icon: mark-skill-book
A book that raises a skill when you read it.

## Sovngarde {#sovngarde}
The Nord afterlife, which you visit near the end of the main quest.

## Thalmor {#thalmor}
The High Elf rulers of the Aldmeri Dominion. They hunt worshippers of Talos and attack some starts.

## Forsworn {#forsworn}
Rebels of the Reach, in the west of Skyrim. In LoreRim they are level 25 to 39.

## Falmer {#falmer}
Blind elves who live in Dwemer ruins and caves underground.

## Dwemer ruin {#dwemer-ruin}
- aka: Dwemer ruins, Dwemer
A ruin of the Dwemer, a vanished race of elves, full of machines and traps.

## Solstheim {#solstheim}
An island north-east of Skyrim, reached by boat from Windhelm. The Dragonborn add-on takes place there.

## Dragon statue {#dragon-statue}
The statue in the Helgen inn where you choose your start.
