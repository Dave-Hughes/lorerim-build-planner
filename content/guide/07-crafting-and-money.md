Crafting in LoreRim gives little or no XP. Smithing perks come from books, potions need empty bottles, and merchants pay little for what you sell.

## Smithing {#smithing}

You can't buy Smithing material perks with perk points. Each one comes from a book: reach the base Smithing level the book needs, hold the perk before it, and read it. Gear or potions that raise Smithing don't count. If you read a book too early, nothing happens and you keep the book for later.

The first book, The Armorer's Encyclopedia, needs Smithing 10, and every blacksmith sells it. It gives Craftsmanship, which you need for almost everything: making iron, steel, silver, wooden and leather gear, tempering it, and tanning hides. Only two Smithing perks cost perk points: Smithing Specialization (60) and Iron Lore (80).

Smelting ore into ingots needs no perk. Breaking looted weapons and armor down into materials is switched off.

| The Art of Dwarven Blacksmithing | Dwarven Smithing | 30 | Calcelmo, Urag |

| Art of the Advanced Nordic Blacksmiths | Advanced Blacksmithing | 30 | Eorlund, Baldor |

- Tailoring & Polishing: make clothing at a tanning rack and polish gems into Flawless gems with sandpaper.
- Advanced Blacksmithing: plate armor, Nordic gear, rings, necklaces and circlets.
- Recipes without a perk: any ingot at a smelter (2 ore, or 1 Iron Ore and 1 Corundum Ore for steel), Leather Strips (1 Leather makes 4), a waterskin, and empty potion bottles (2 Glass makes 3).

:::ask{q="I can't unlock a Smithing perk"}
Check three things: your base Smithing (not counting gear) is at the book's level, you have the perk the book builds on, and you have read the book. Reading it too early does nothing; read it again later.
:::

## Tempering {#tempering}

Tempering improves a weapon at a grindstone or armor at a workbench, for one unit of its material. The item gets a quality name in front: Well-Made, High-Grade, First-Rate, Exquisite, Master Work, Legendary. The higher your Smithing, the bigger the bonus. Re-tempering later replaces the old bonus.

You need the perk for the item's material. Requiem's changelog says the game can't temper anything below Smithing 14.

Any blacksmith will also temper your gear for gold: choose the repair option in their dialogue. How good the result is depends on the smith's own skill.

:::more
- A temper can add up to 60 points of damage or armor.
- The smith's price: 100 gold per quality step, plus the item's value multiplied by its new quality (1.1 to 1.6). A Steel Quarterstaff (value 80) to High-Grade costs 296 gold.
- Gear wears down with use, faster with power attacks, but never breaks. Temper it again to repair it.
:::

:::mods
- **Temper and Recharge Services**: Paying smiths to temper.
  - where: `MCM/Settings/Temper&Recharge.ini`
- **Equipment Durability System NG**: Gear wearing down.
:::

## Alchemy {#alchemy}

You need an Empty Potion Bottle for each potion or poison you brew, and at least one to use an alchemy lab at all. Drinking a potion gives you its bottle back. Apothecaries sell bottles, and a forge makes 3 from 2 Glass.

Most races brew nothing useful until they take the first Alchemy perk, Alchemical Lore. Argonians, Bretons, Khajiit and Bosmer can brew without it. Everyone's potions start weaker than in Skyrim without mods, and the perks bring them back up.

To learn what an ingredient does, eat it (first effect), pay an alchemist 15 gold per unknown effect, or take Alchemical Lore rank 2 (Alchemy 20) to learn all four by eating. At a lab, press `\` (backslash) to list every recipe you can make from effects you know.

At a cooking pot, 3 weak potions of one kind make 1 of the next strength: Depleted, Diluted, Faint, Fair, Good, Remarkable.

:::more
- Potion strength: everyone ×0.5. Alchemical Lore ×1.5, Improved Elixirs (25) ×1.25. Fortify potions for the five magic schools, One-Handed, Two-Handed, Archery and Block are halved again; paralysis poisons ×0.25.
- Glass: 3 empty bottles make 1 Glass at a smelter. Glass is sold by general goods merchants.
- Each potion brewed gives 5 XP.
:::

:::mods
- **Alchemy Requires Bottles Redux**: Potions need bottles.
- **Requiem - Alchemy Redone**: Potion strength and the race rule.
- **Alchemical Appraisal Services**: Paying alchemists to identify ingredients.
- **Alchemy recipe view mode SE**: The recipe list key.
- **Requiem - Potion Merging**: Combining potions at a cooking pot.
:::

:::ask{q="The game froze after my last potion"}
It isn't frozen. Press Space. You have run out of bottles; get more before brewing again.
:::

## Cooking {#cooking}

Cooking pots, spits and many campfire kettles let you cook. Cooked food gives bonuses (see [Food and drink](/guide/staying-alive#food)). You can also brew ale and mead at a cooking pot: Ale is 2 Wheat and 1 Empty Potion Bottle. Cooking gives no XP.

## Mining, hunting and wood {#gathering}

A pickaxe is in every blacksmith's stock and in most mines. One swing at an ore vein gives 3 ore and empties it, and each vein you empty gives 10 XP. Veins refill after 30 game days.

Hides and pelts become leather at a tanning rack. That needs Craftsmanship, except for skeever pelts. Pelts also sell to hunters.

Mill owners such as Hod in Riverwood buy firewood at 2 gold a log.

:::more
| Hide or pelt | Leather |
| --- | --- |
| Bear, sabre cat, mammoth fur, werewolf pelt | 4 |
| Cow, horse, troll (Requiem's troll hide) | 3 |
| Deer, elk, Animal Pelt | 2 |
| Goat, wolf, hare, skeever | 1 |
| Fox (2 pelts) | 1 |

- Mule hide has no tanning recipe.
- A cleared location's veins refill after 300 days.
- Ore values: Iron 2, Corundum 20, Silver 25, Ebony 60, Gold 100.
:::

:::mods
- **Faster Mining Plus SE**: One swing per vein.
- **Simple Hunting Overhaul**: Animal Pelts and hunting.
:::

## Buying and selling {#trade}

Merchants charge about four times an item's value and pay about a quarter of it. Speech and the Speech perks narrow the gap, but you never pay less than double or get more than half. Each merchant has a limited amount of gold, refilled every 7 days, so sell across several towns.

Each shop buys only its own kind of goods until you take the Speech perk Merchant (50), after which any merchant buys anything that isn't stolen. Stolen goods go to Thieves Guild fences.

:::more
| Merchant | Typical gold |
| --- | --- |
| Street vendor | 40 to 100 |
| Inn | 110 to 210 |
| Apothecary | 160 to 280 |
| General goods | 220 to 360 |
| Blacksmith | 290 to 450 |
| Spell merchant | 370 to 550 |
| Fence | 200 |

- Haggling: prices 1% better per Speech level. Silver Tongue (25): 10% better. Merchant (50): 20% better, sell anything, invest 250 gold to raise a merchant's gold by 100. Fencing (100): sell stolen goods to anyone.
- At Speech 20 a Steel Quarterstaff (value 80) costs about 300 gold and sells for about 21.
- A fence can remove the stolen mark from an item for a fee.
:::

:::mods
- **[LoreRim] Economy Overhaul**: Prices, merchant gold and restock time.
- **Sanctified Stolen Goods**: Fences removing the stolen mark.
:::

:::ask{q="Why do merchants pay so little?"}
Merchants pay about a quarter of the value at low Speech, and never more than half. Gems and jewelry are light and worth the most for their weight.
:::

## Work for gold {#work}

- **Day jobs:** ask farmers, smiths, innkeepers, miners, lumberjacks and others for work. Six game hours pass and you are paid by how well you did, up to several hundred gold. Day jobs give no XP.
- **Missives:** gold as well as 100 XP each (see [Jobs that pay XP](/guide/first-days#jobs)).
- **Jarl bounties:** 250 gold for a bandit or Forsworn leader, 600 for a giant, 1,000 for a dragon, and no XP. The same targets taken as missives also pay 100 XP.
- **Thieves Guild jobs:** 200 gold and 100 XP each.
- **Playing an instrument at an inn:** small tips once you are good enough.

:::more
- Day job pay by result: poor, nothing; sufficient, 25 to 100; well, 100 to 250; great, 250 to 500; amazing, 500 to 750 and an item.
- An employer has a 20% chance to have no work for you; ask again after 8 hours.
- No tools are needed for day jobs.
:::

:::mods
- **Andrealletius' Jobs Overhaul (AJO)**: Day jobs.
- **Skyrim's Got Talent**: Performing at inns.
:::

## Homes {#homes}

Containers that aren't yours, including those in inn rooms, can empty themselves after 30 game days. Your own house is safe, and so are your horse's saddlebags and your followers' packs while they live.

| Home | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Breezehome, Whiterun | 5,000 |
| Honeyside, Riften | 8,000 |
| Vlindrel Hall, Markarth | 8,000 |
| Hjerim, Windhelm | 12,000 |
| Proudspire Manor, Solitude | 25,000 |
| Hearthfire land (Lakeview, Windstad, Heljarchen) | 5,000 each |
| Severin Manor, Raven Rock | 10,000 |

There is no free house at the start.

:::more
- Hearthfire: the small house 1,000; rooms 1,000 to 3,500.
- Severin Manor is sold by Cindiri Arano after Served Cold.
- Creation Club homes come after their quests: Bloodchill Manor after Bloodline, Hendraheim after The Silver Hand, Gallows Hall after the Conjuration ritual spell quest.
:::
