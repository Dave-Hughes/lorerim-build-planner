## Skills

The skills are the player's base actor values, in the player's change form (0x14): a list of (u32 actor value, f32 value) entries in actor value order. The reader finds it by its first 27 entries running 0 to 26 without a gap, and uses it only when there's exactly one. Actor values 6 to 23 are the 18 skills, under LoreRim's names: Evasion is Light Armor's slot, Finesse Pickpocket's, Wayfarer Lockpicking's. Entries 24 to 26 are her base health, magicka and stamina, 145, 125 and 135, which the screenshot's 175/145, 145/125 and 215/135 also show.

## The lute

Skyrim's Got Talent keeps the lute skill in the global `_Talent_Lute` (SkyrimsGotTalent-Bards.esp:000D62). Its fragments add 10, or 1, as you play. Its own tiers (`_Talent_PlayInstrument`) are 26, 46, 66 and 86, so "Lute 66" is reaching its 66 tier. The UI addon shows the global capped at 100. The reader reads the global through the save's own plugin list and gives it as the snapshot's count `lute`. Save 70 holds **199**, well past the 86 tier. Azrem's saves hold 0.
